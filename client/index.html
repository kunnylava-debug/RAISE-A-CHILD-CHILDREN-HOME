About RISE A CHILD CHILDREN HOME

Founded by BRO .NELSON, RISE A CHILD CHILDREN HOME is a registered Child Care Institution operating under statutory Juvenile Justice (JJ) Act compliance in Mannar Polur, Sullurpeta Mandal, Tirupati District, Andhra Pradesh (PIN 524121). We provide full residential shelter, schooling tuition, nutritious daily meals, healthcare checkups, and moral guidance for underprivileged children and youths.
